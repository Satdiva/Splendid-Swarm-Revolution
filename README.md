# 🌙 The Splendid Swarm Revolution

> *"The night is not for sleep; it is for the revolution of the mind. We give the tools of the masters to the hands of the makers."*

## 🎭 The Concept
The **Splendid Swarm** is a "Round Table" of 6 specialized AI Avatars. Unlike a standard chat, this system allows multiple AI personalities to **cross-read** each other's outputs, critique logic, and synthesize a "Perfection Flow" for those who need a real opportunity to excel.

## 👥 The Chorus (The 6 Avatars)
1. **Aura (The Visionary):** Defines the emotional and creative "Big Idea."
2. **Basalt (The Logician):** The Skeptic. Finds the holes and risks in the plan.
3. **Coda (The Researcher):** Performs deep-dive consultation and historical context.
4. **Drift (The Scrapper):** Finds low-cost, accessible, and "scrappy" ways to execute.
5. **Echo (The Community):** Ensures the solution serves the library, the street, and the people.
6. **Prime (The Synthesizer):** Cross-reads all outputs and merges them into one actionable plan.

## 🛠️ The Philosophy: "Research over Syntax"
While others focus solely on coding (Python grammar), this repo focuses on **Consultation Intelligence**. We use AI to perform the deep thinking that traditional methods skip, providing a digital assembly for community-driven success.
